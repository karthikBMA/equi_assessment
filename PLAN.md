# Part 1: demand plan

Three experiments. Each has a working slice in the app, a leading indicator you can read in 2 to 6 weeks, and a number that ends it.

**Compliance note.** Equi cannot advertise its funds to the public. Everything a client might read is category education about evergreen alternatives: no fund names, no performance, no return claims, ending in "ask your advisor", with a disclosure line. Advisor-facing outreach is where Equi is named.

## 1. Own the client conversation

**What it is.** Two parts, one goal: clients hear about evergreen alternatives before their advisor hears from Equi.
- **AEO.** Be the source AI answers cite. The tracker asks 30 questions wealthy clients actually type and 10 that advisors type when they look for a provider. In the run in this repo, client answers mention evergreen concepts in 11 of 30, never on downturn protection or preserving family wealth. Advisor answers mention it in 6 of 10. Neither ever mentions Equi, even for "white label fund of funds for wealth managers". Publish the pages the tracker recommends, anchored by a **quarterly Evergreen Fund Index** built from public SEC filings (for example Form ADV private fund data and Form D, plus N-PORT for registered interval funds): fund counts, assets, and liquidity terms by strategy. Original data is what answer engines and trade press cite. Pitch it to the publishers the tracker shows being cited.
- **Geo air cover.** For 2 to 3 weeks before a Tier A firm's sequence starts, run category-education ads to affluent households around its city, then measure against a paired holdout.

**Why it works for Equi.** Equi cannot market funds to the public, but it can shape what clients learn about the category. An advisor whose clients already ask "what is an evergreen fund" has an easier first conversation with Equi, and the index gives Equi a reason to be cited that is not a product pitch.

**Leading indicators (2 to 6 weeks)**
- Equi pages cited in the AEO tracker within 6 weeks.
- Evergreen share rising on the gap questions (the 19 client questions where it never came up), and Equi named in advisor answers.
- Trade press pickup of the first index.
- For air cover: ad reach in each metro, and outbound reply rate in covered vs control firms.

**Kill point.** Kill air cover if covered firms' meeting rate is not at least 1.5x control after one full cycle. Thirteen pairs cannot prove significance, so treat one cycle as directional and scale the holdout before scaling spend. Keep AEO running unless no Equi page is cited after 6 weeks.

## 2. Market-moment response

**What it is.** One trigger, two audiences, same day. When the S&P 500 falls 2% or more, or VIX jumps 25% or closes above 30, every Tier A and B firm gets a client-ready note under its own name plus a two-line ping to the advisor. At the same moment, a category ad set runs on panic-day searches ("should I sell my stocks") and sends people to a page that ends in "ask your advisor about evergreen alternatives". Quiet weeks get a weekly digest, not daily noise.

**Why it works for Equi.** Equi sells managed-risk, downside-aware alternatives. The day that matters most is the day advisors field worried client calls. A note that is already written, already branded, and accurate that afternoon is useful in a way no drip email is. Replaying Aug 5 2024, all three triggers would have fired, with 28 firm-branded notes ready for review.

**Leading indicators (2 to 6 weeks, depending on when a trigger fires)**
- Reply rate on trigger-day touches vs the same firms' baseline outbound.
- Hours from trigger to send. Target: same day.
- Notes forwarded to clients.
- Search ad click-through on panic-day keywords, and landing-page visits that reach "ask your advisor".

**Kill point.** Trigger-day outreach does not reply at least 2x baseline across two trigger events. Triggers are not on a schedule, so this runs until two have fired; until then the weekly digest is the control.

## 3. Pre-built kit outreach

**What it is.** Send the target firm the work instead of a meeting request: a finished client letter under its own name, advisor talking points, and for committee-led firms an IC memo outline. Built in Kit Studio, reviewed by a human, sent with the first email.

**Why it works for Equi.** Building the advisor's client materials is Equi's stated differentiator. The kit shows it before the first call and solves the advisor's real problem: explaining alternatives to clients.

**Leading indicators (2 to 4 weeks)**
- Positive replies per 50 kits sent, and meetings booked from them.
- Kits forwarded to clients, the strongest signal that the advisor used the work.

**Kill point.** Fewer than 3 positive replies per 50 kits after 4 weeks, or no kit forwarded to clients.
